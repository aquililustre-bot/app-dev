
# Five Feet Apart (2019)

## My Favorite Movie

**Five Feet Apart** is one of my favorite romantic movies. It tells the story of two teenagers, Stella Grant and Will Newman, who meet in a hospital while receiving treatment for cystic fibrosis. Even though they fall in love, they must keep a safe distance from each other to protect their health.

### Main Characters
- **Stella Grant** – A responsible girl who always follows her treatment routine.
- **Will Newman** – A young man who is tired of living with his illness and wants to enjoy life.

### Movie Details
- **Title:** Five Feet Apart
- **Release Year:** 2019
- **Genre:** Romance, Drama
- **Director:** Justin Baldoni
- **Main Actors:** Haley Lu Richardson and Cole Sprouse

### Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because it shows how powerful love can be, even when two people cannot be physically close to each other. It also teaches us to appreciate life, value the people around us, and never take small moments for granted.

### My Favorite Lesson

> Love is not always about being close to someone. Sometimes, it means caring for them even when you have to stay apart.

### Movie Rating

**My Rating: 10/10** ⭐
